I graduated from MIT in 2026 with a degree in architecture and minors in Entrepreneurship & Innovation and Art, Culture, & Technology.

Most of my work starts with people: the neighbors who shared a hill with my family, the teammates I biked across the country with, the students in the fabrication workshops I teach. I’m drawn to memory and nostalgia, and to the way a room or an object can hold them.

I like making things with my hands, and I move between drawing, models, film, photography and graphics, often within one project. This room is where all of it lives.
